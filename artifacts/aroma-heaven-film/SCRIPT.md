# Aroma Heaven Spa — Booking-First Film

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** A private Umhlanga sanctuary where comfort, discretion, and a moment of stillness come first.
- **Identity:** Brand pink (#d10b83, #e01c91) against ink (#171516, #201c1e), softened by warm ivory (#f7f3ed). Playfair Display carries the intimate, editorial phrases; Montserrat holds crisp details.
- **Hero:** The spa's own photography, treated as tactile moving planes rather than stock backdrops; the official lotus wordmark is reserved for the ending.
- **Motion:** Close photographic push-throughs with matched crops; masked editorial typography; thin magenta rules that grow into image frames.
- **Arc:** Start with immediate human closeness, move from calm professional care into the more atmospheric collections, then exhale into a clean ivory booking-information lockup.
- **Payoff:** The film closes in bright ivory with the official logo and actual Umhlanga enquiry details; the opening returns through the pink lotus mark as a graphic match.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.

## Variety and production review

Five beats progress from close human photo, to a clean split collection composition, to red silhouette aperture, to an asymmetric private portrait, and finally a bright restrained end card. Adjacent shots change framing and dominant mechanic; image details and the pink line/ring provide continuity.
